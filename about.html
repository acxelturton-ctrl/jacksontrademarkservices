About Jackson Trademark Services

JTS is a family-run company formed in 2009 to fill a gap we saw in the traffic control industry. We may be a newer name in the field, but our team carries many years of hands-on experience, and we've built JTS around fresh, practical approaches to traffic control.

Safety comes first, full stop. Whether you're dealing with our senior management or one of our certified traffic control operatives on-site, that Safety First philosophy holds. The goal on every job is that everyone stays safe and no one gets hurt.

Our crews are ready to work anywhere in Ontario, and we relish the chance to talk through your traffic control needs and show that a safe work zone doesn't have to cost the earth. Contact us today. Your budget will be glad you did.
