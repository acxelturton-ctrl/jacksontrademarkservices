Traffic control services in Barrie, Ontario

JTS supplies certified traffic control crews, planning and equipment for utility, construction and municipal work zones throughout Barrie and Simcoe County.

JTS crews manage lane closures and merge tapers along Barrie-area highway corridors.

Whether it's a scheduled utility shutdown, a construction lane closure, or an emergency call-out, JTS crews are ready to deploy in Barrie with certified Traffic Control Persons, signed and equipped trucks, and site-specific traffic control plans built to OTM Book 7 standards.

We regularly work alongside major utility and construction contractors across Barrie and neighbouring communities like Innisfil, Orillia and cottage country, so your site stays compliant and your crews stay safe from the first cone to the last.
