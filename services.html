Traffic control services & equipment

A full breakdown of the crews, equipment and paperwork JTS provides for non-freeway and freeway work zones across the GTA and York Region. Exact crew size and equipment depend on your site's traffic control plan.

Non-Freeway

Non-freeway traffic control

Crew and equipment options for municipal roads, arterials and local work zones.

Standard Traffic Crew
Two Traffic Control Persons, truck & equipment.
Alternative #1
One Traffic Control Person, truck, equipment & TC-12 arrow board.
Alternative #2
One Traffic Control Person, truck and equipment (no TC-12).
Flagging Crew
Two Traffic Control Persons, truck & equipment.
Additional TCP
An extra Traffic Control Person added to an existing crew.
Freeway

Freeway traffic control

Higher-speed corridors call for dedicated crash protection alongside standard crews.

Standard Traffic Crew
Two Traffic Control Persons, truck & equipment.
Crash Truck
Crash truck & operator only, positioned to protect the work zone.
Rental Equipment

Equipment rental

Select equipment available to rent for sites that need to stay compliant between crew visits.

TC-12
Arrow board trailer.
