Traffic control services for OE Utility Services projects

JTS supplies certified Local 183 union Traffic Control Persons, signage and site-specific plans for OE Utility Services' hydro excavation, water & gas distribution, and underground utility work across the GTA & York Region.

Ontario's full-solution hydro excavation & utility services provider · celebrating 30 years in business.

OE Utility Services (formerly Ontario Excavac) is one of Ontario's leading hydro excavation and underground utility support companies, handling hydrovac digs, keyhole coring, and water and gas distribution work for utility, construction and engineering customers throughout the GTA. Every excavation site OE opens needs a safe, compliant traffic control zone around it. That's where JTS comes in.

Our Local 183 union Traffic Control Persons work alongside OE's crews on hydrovac and utility jobs of every size, from single keyhole digs on quiet residential streets to longer-duration excavation and restoration projects on busier roads. We build the site-specific traffic control plan, hold the closure for as long as OE's crew needs it, and can mobilize on short notice when a job comes up unexpectedly.

About the Company

OE Utility Services started in Mississauga with a single hydrovac truck. Over three decades, the company grew into Ontario's full-solution utility services provider for gas, water and subsurface utility engineering customers, now operating from seven locations across the province, including Vaughan, Kitchener, Oshawa, Ottawa and London.

Today OE is headquartered in Vaughan, Ontario and holds COR 2020 and ISO 45001 certifications, with recognition as a Safe Employer by SOSE. The company also runs the only on-premises soil processing and recycling facility of its kind in Ontario, cutting truck travel time and carbon emissions on every job.
