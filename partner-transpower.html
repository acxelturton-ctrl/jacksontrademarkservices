Traffic control services for TransPower projects

JTS provides traffic control support for TransPower's power line and utility infrastructure work, keeping crews and drivers safe around live electrical work zones.

JTS supplies certified traffic control crews for TransPower line and utility work.

Power line construction, pole replacement and overhead utility work often means partial or full closures on both quiet residential streets and busier arterial roads. TransPower crews rely on JTS to hold a safe, compliant work zone so their linespeople can focus on the job overhead rather than the traffic below.

From bucket truck setups that need a rolling closure to longer-duration pole and conductor work that runs over several days, our Local 183 union Traffic Control Persons handle lane closures, detours and flagging to OTM Book 7 standards, with TC-12 arrow boards and full signage on hand.
