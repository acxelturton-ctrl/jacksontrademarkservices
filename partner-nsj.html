Traffic control services for NSJ projects

JTS provides certified traffic control support for NSJ's infrastructure and utility projects across the GTA & York Region.

JTS supplies certified traffic control crews for NSJ infrastructure projects.

Infrastructure and utility projects often run on tight municipal timelines with specific permit and notification requirements. NSJ works with JTS to make sure the traffic control side of the job (plans, permits, signage and crew) is handled so field work can stay on schedule.

We build the site-specific traffic control plan up front, secure the necessary road-occupancy permits and notifications, and keep certified Traffic Control Persons on site for the length of the project.
