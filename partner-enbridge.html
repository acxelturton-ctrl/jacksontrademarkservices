Traffic control services for Enbridge projects

JTS supplies certified Traffic Control Persons, signage and site-specific plans for Enbridge's gas distribution and pipeline maintenance work across the GTA & York Region.

JTS supplies certified traffic control crews for Enbridge gas distribution & pipeline projects.

Enbridge crews working on gas main installation, service line replacement and pipeline integrity digs need traffic control that keeps residential streets and arterial roads moving safely around active excavation. JTS has supported Enbridge work zones throughout the GTA & York Region, from single-lane residential closures to larger main replacement projects that span multiple blocks.

Our Local 183 union Traffic Control Persons are trained to work alongside gas crews and understand the specific safety considerations that come with excavation near live gas infrastructure, from exclusion zones to coordinated signal timing with municipal traffic staff. We supply signage, cones, arrow boards and TCPs on the schedule Enbridge crews need, including short-notice and emergency call-outs.
