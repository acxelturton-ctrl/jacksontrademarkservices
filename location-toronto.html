Traffic control services in Toronto, Ontario

JTS supplies certified traffic control crews, planning and equipment for utility, construction and municipal work zones throughout Toronto and its surrounding boroughs.

Highway 401 · TorontoGTA

Whether it's a scheduled utility shutdown, a construction lane closure, or an emergency call-out, JTS crews are ready to deploy in Toronto with certified Traffic Control Persons, signed and equipped trucks, and site-specific traffic control plans built to OTM Book 7 standards.
