JTS · Site Safety

Pedestrian & Worker Safety

Dedicated watch for pedestrians, cyclists and crew, spotting hazards before they become incidents. Every JTS Traffic Control Person is trained to manage sightlines, signage and foot traffic around an active work zone, not just vehicle traffic.

What's Included

Crew & equipment options

Exact crew size and equipment depend on your site's traffic control plan.

Pedestrian Routing
Signed, physically separated pedestrian routes around excavation, laydown areas and equipment.
Cyclist Awareness
Sightline and signage management at conflict points where cyclists cross or run alongside the work zone.
Crew Spotting
A dedicated set of eyes watching for equipment blind spots, reversing vehicles and shifting site conditions.
High-Visibility Standards
All TCPs equipped and trained to current high-visibility apparel and positioning standards.

Extra charges for equipment and truck may apply if required. Travel time, minimum charges and fuel charges quoted as required.
